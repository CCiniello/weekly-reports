1Executive summary
Angular paid spend rose to $1,991.74 (+21.4% WoW) and impressions jumped +50% to 2,021 — but the growth is hollow. Nearly all the added impressions came from the DemandGen campaign (450→1,244 impressions), which dragged blended CTR from 6.68% down to 5.15%. The high-intent Search campaigns actually served fewer impressions than last week (897→777) at a richer $22.81 search CPC. Two problems dominate this week: (1) conversions are zero in both Google Ads and GA4 — no trial/SDK events are being counted, so CPL is undefined and we are flying blind; and (2) only 5 of 104 paid clicks show up as google/cpc sessions on the Angular pages, meaning paid traffic is not landing where GA4 can see it. Search impression share also slipped 9.7pp to 83.1%, now lost mostly to budget (14.67%, up from 0.9%). Verdict: FIX before scale — see Site Behavior tab.

4What’s working / what’s not
Workingkeep doing
Search intent quality is high. Search-only CTR is 10.8% and the Components campaign hit 13.1% CTR on a $19.71 CPC. Charts CPC fell hard ($31.63→$16.60) while clicks rose. The handful of google/cpc sessions that do reach Angular pages engage deeply (260s avg, 40% engagement). Not workingfix now
Measurement is dark. Zero conversions in Ads and GA4 — trial/SDK key events are not firing or not imported. Paid clicks aren’t landing on /ignite-ui-angular (5 cpc sessions vs 104 clicks), so we can’t see what the money buys. DemandGen is diluting the account — spend nearly 4×’d to $75.95 for 1.61% CTR. Budget cap now costs 14.67% of search impression share, and the Compete campaign’s IS collapsed from 96.7% to 65.9%.
1Where the budget went
Budget was re-weighted hard into Grids this week — spend went $277→$681 (+146%) and CPC climbed to $27.25, the most expensive line in the account. That came at the expense of Compete, which we cut 30% and immediately lost visibility on (impression share 96.7%→65.9%). Components held steady as the efficiency leader (13.1% CTR, $19.71 CPC). None of the five campaigns recorded a tracked conversion, so “where it converts” cannot be answered from Ads data — budget concentration is currently a bet on intent, not on measured return.

Verdict · Fix, then scale

FIX before any scale. This account is spending $1,992/week at a $19 CPC into a measurement black box — zero conversions in Ads and GA4, and only 5 of 104 paid clicks traceable to the Angular pages. Adding budget now just buys more clicks we can’t evaluate. Two fixes, in order: (1) stand up conversion tracking — fire and import the trial-start / SDK-download / docs key events so both platforms count them; (2) audit the ad final URLs and auto-tagging — paid clicks are clearly landing off /ignite-ui-angular (or losing their gclid), so align destinations and confirm GCLID pass-through. Only after those are green should we act on the genuine demand signal: the Search core (angular table, component library, ui components) runs 90%+ impression share at 13–16% CTR and is now budget-capped (14.67% lost IS). At that point, reallocate the ~$300–$470/wk of competitor/Material close-variant waste and the diluting DemandGen spend into those capped core terms, and restore Compete’s budget to recover its lost 30pp of impression share. Net: Fix tracking, cut the leakage, hold spend flat this week — scale next week on real signal.
