1How healthy is Angular paid?
Two truths this week. On the surface, spend rose to $1,991.74 (+21.4% WoW) and the high-intent Search core is genuinely strong — 10.8% search CTR, 90%+ impression share on our category terms. Underneath, the account is running blind and shrinking. Conversions are zero in Google Ads and GA4, so nothing downstream can be judged on paid’s own numbers. Worse, paid volume to Angular pages has collapsed: GA4 shows ~17k cpc sessions/month in Apr–May, then 788 in June and 48 in July — a spring Display/PMax/DemandGen program was switched off and only the ~$1.9k/wk Search lines remain. Attribution is broken too: 15,059 of ~20,000 paid sessions carry a literal {campaignname} UTM placeholder that never resolved. Where do we focus this week? Not on scaling. Fix the two measurement breaks, cut competitor/Material leakage, and hold spend — then scale the budget-capped core. The one bright spot that needs no fixing: npm installs of igniteui-angular are climbing (35,516 in June, +10% MoM), so developer adoption is healthy even while paid is dark.

Verdict · Fix & reallocate — do not scale yet

FIX, then reallocate. The Angular account is spending ~$2k/week at a $19 CPC into a measurement black box — zero tracked conversions, 75% of sessions untagged, and every ad/creative asset ungraded because Google has no signal. You cannot scale what you cannot see. Step one (this week): stand up conversion tracking (fire & import trial-start / docs / SDK-install key events) and fix auto-tagging so {campaignname} resolves — this alone unblocks Ad Copy grading, Creative grading, and the whole funnel.

Step two: cut the ~$300–$470/wk of competitor & Angular-Material close-variant leakage, add the four exact-match gaps, and restore Compete’s budget to recover its lost 30pp of impression share. Step three: decide on the wound-down Display/PMax program — it drove 17k mobile sessions/month that never showed adoption, so reallocating that budget into the budget-capped desktop Search core is the higher-ROI move. Hold total spend flat this week. Adoption (npm +40% since Jan) proves the product is winning developers; the job now is to make paid provably contribute to it. Scale next week, on real signal.
