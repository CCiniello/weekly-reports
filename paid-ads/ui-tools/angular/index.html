1 Executive read

Angular paid is spending steadily but flying blind. The account put $1,961 to work this week (+13.3% WoW) across four Search themes and one DemandGen line, earned 103 clicks at a 5.4% blended CTR — and recorded zero conversions, because conversion tracking is not wired to Ads. That is the headline problem: we cannot judge ROI from the ad platform at all.

What's working: Grids and Charts own their real estate (91.9% and 96.6% search impression share) and Grids doubled spend into strong 11.5% CTR demand. What's breaking: the Compete campaign was throttled — spend cut 30% and impression share collapsed from 96.7% to 66.2%, now losing 32% of impressions to budget. And the GA4 paid picture cratered: cpc sessions to Angular pages fell from 17.7K in May to 49 so far in July as a PMax/Display program wound down, leaving Search clicks landing under a broken {campaignname} tag.

Focus this week: (1) fix conversion tracking + auto-tagging so the funnel is legible, (2) restore Compete budget to recapture the 32% we're forfeiting on competitor terms, and (3) add negatives to stop ~$505/mo bleeding into free-framework "angular material / angular library" queries. Adoption — the real endpoint — is healthy: igniteui-angular npm installs hit 13,522 this week, +109% WoW.

Verdict — Fix before scale

Keep the campaigns running — but do not scale a funnel you can't measure. The demand is real: Grids and Charts own their real estate, Search CTR is a strong 11%, and igniteui-angular adoption is at record highs. But Angular paid is measurement-blind — 0 conversion tracking and a {campaignname} tagging break that hides ~90% of paid sessions — so no ROI claim is defensible today. Three fixes, in order: (1) wire conversion tracking (trial starts / SDK signups) so Ads can optimize and asset grading unlocks; (2) repair auto-tagging/UTM to make the funnel legible; (3) restore Compete budget and add the "angular material" / "angular library" negatives to reclaim ~$505/mo and stop forfeiting 32% of competitor-term impressions. Re-evaluate for scale-up in 2 weeks once conversions are flowing. Reallocate now: shift the ~$505/mo of junk spend into Grids and Charts, the two themes already converting attention into engaged sessions.
