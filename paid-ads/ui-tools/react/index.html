Report date Mon Jul 27, 2026 · WoW window Jul 20–26 vs Jul 13–19 · npm MoM · One metric, one tool

The story this week

The account is spending confidently and flying blind. React paid put $4,768 to work this week (up 19.6% WoW) and bought 1,769 clicks (up 24.9%) — but Google Ads recorded just 1 conversion, and GA4 saw only a trickle of paid sessions. This is not a demand problem. It is a measurement break: GA4 google/cpc sessions on infragistics.com fell off a cliff after May — 24,599 in May → 1,573 in June → 283 so far in July — while spend kept climbing. Engagement on the sessions we do capture is healthy (63.5%), so traffic quality is fine; we simply stopped counting it. Until this is fixed, CPA/CPL is unknowable and every asset sits ungraded ("Pending") because Google has no conversion signal to optimize against.

What's working. The Search-Compete fund shipped last week did exactly what we bet on: budget-lost-abs-top dropped from 36%→11%, absolute-top IS climbed 45%→54%, and search IS jumped to 77%. Compete is now our strongest-positioned React campaign. Search creative is also strong where it's served — top RSAs are pulling 18–22% CTR on high-intent grid/component queries.

Where we focus this week. One: fix tracking — nothing else is trustworthy until sessions and conversions flow again. Two: "react grid" rank on Search-Grids lost 71% of absolute-top to Ad Rank (up from 60%) on quality scores of 2–4 — a Quality Score problem, not a budget one. Three: contain DemandGen React, which quietly doubled to $750 with 2 GA4 sessions and zero tracked conversions.

What we can still say

Top-of-funnel is strong and improving: spend efficient (CPC $2.70, down WoW), CTR up, Compete winning its auction, RSAs pulling 18–22%. Where GA4 does capture sessions, engagement is 63.5% — this is quality traffic. The problem is entirely one of measurement and attribution, not demand or creative.

Verdict

FIX — then hold spend flat

Do not scale on numbers we can't trust. Freeze net-new budget (except the small Charts top-up), repair tracking + conversion import, and re-baseline CPA/CPL next week. In parallel, fix "react grid" Quality Score and cap Demand Gen. Reallocate nothing until the funnel reports again.
