2 Shared paid-media terms
Impression Share (IS)
% of impressions won vs eligible. Abs-top IS = share of absolute-top (first) slot.
Budget-lost / Rank-lost IS
Share missed because budget was too low (budget) or Ad Rank too low (rank/Quality Score).
Quality Score (QS)
1–10 relevance grade (expected CTR, ad relevance, LP experience). Low QS → high rank-lost IS.
CTR / CPC
Click-through rate; cost per click. Blended = across a campaign set, per channel (never cross-channel).
Demand Gen
Google's image/audience prospecting channel. High volume, low intent — needs its own efficiency guardrails.
Negatives
Excluded queries. Here: OSS libraries (react-flow, xyflow, echarts) with no buying intent for Ignite UI.
