A Paid glossary
IS (Impression Share) — impressions received ÷ impressions eligible. Low IS = you're missing auctions.
Abs-top IS — share of times your ad hit the very first slot above results. The premium position.
Budget-lost abs-top — top-slot impressions missed because budget ran out. Fix = more budget.
Rank-lost abs-top — top-slot impressions missed due to Ad Rank (bid × Quality). Fix = QS/bid, not budget.
QS (Quality Score) — 1–10 relevance grade (expected CTR, ad relevance, LP experience). Low QS raises CPC.
CTR — clicks ÷ impressions. Search health signal; feeds QS.
CPC — cost per click. Rises when QS falls or competition heats up.
Demand Gen — Google's visual/discovery format (image/video). Upper-funnel, lower intent than search.
Negatives — keywords you block to stop paying for irrelevant queries.
Auction Insights — competitor overlap/position data. Not exposed by our current Google Ads data source (confirmed via field_discovery) — genuinely unavailable, not just disconnected.
B "Ours" — product terms
NPM installs — our north-star adoption metric (weekly package downloads across igniteui-react/-core/-grids/-charts). Blended signal, not 1:1 paid-attributed.
By-package split — igniteui-react + -core + -grids + -charts, reported summed and separately.
No conversion event, by design — React's trial download is ungated (no form fill), so there is no lead/conversion signal to track. We optimize toward website traffic and engagement, not CPA/CPL. Retired: "flying blind" / "conversion tracking broken" language.
Google vs Bing — always reported side by side, never summed into one figure (different auctions, different tools). When one connector is unavailable, we say so rather than guessing.
