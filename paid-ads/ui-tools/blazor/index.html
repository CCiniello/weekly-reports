• Cheat sheet — paid-media glossary

Impressions & share

Impression share (IS)
Impressions you got ÷ impressions you were eligible for. Target 90%.
Budget-lost IS
Share missed because the campaign ran out of budget. High = add budget.
Rank-lost IS
Share missed due to Ad Rank (bid × Quality Score). High = fix relevance/QS, not budget.
Absolute top
The very first paid slot above organic results.

Cost & response

CTR
Clicks ÷ impressions. Relevance signal.
CPC
Average cost per click.
CPA
Cost per acquisition/conversion — n/a here until tracking is wired.
Quality Score
Google's 1–10 relevance rating; lifts rank and cuts CPC.

Match & intent

Exact / Phrase / Broad
How tightly a keyword matches a query. We add gaps as Exact.
Conquest
Bidding on a competitor's brand term (MudBlazor, Radzen, Telerik).
Negative keyword
A term we block to stop wasted spend.
RSA
Responsive Search Ad — Google mixes headlines/descriptions automatically.
