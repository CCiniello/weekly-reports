2 Where Reveal wins the click
True SDK, no iFrameswin theme
Reviewers repeatedly cite native white-label embedding as the deciding factor — the newest 5★ reviewer calls it a "seamless embedded BI SDK… it feels seamless for our users," and another: "our analytics look and feel as if we did it ourselves… no iFrame, no redirect." This is Reveal's sharpest wedge against bolt-on BI. Lean into it in the profile tagline and comparison content.
Flat pricing at scalewin theme
"Flat-rate pricing was key when evaluating against the competition"; one Peru reviewer switched over "a significantly lower price tag… the cost saving was astronomical," and another cites no per-user licensing. Multiple reviewers switched from Tableau over usage-based cost — a live conquest angle against every consumption-priced rival.
Fast deploy · on-prem · regulated fitwin theme
Deploys in "weeks vs a year" (one reviewer: "about a month to deploy"); runs fully on customer infrastructure with no external connections — the reason regulated buyers (fintech, security) pick it. Strong Enterprise proof despite low volume.

Verdict

G2 is a proof asset, not a demand engine — but the proof engine just restarted. Reveal keeps the best rating in its category and glowing switched-from-Tableau reviews, and after seven months idle, three new reviews landed this week — the one G2 lever we control is finally moving. With intent still gated (retested, no access), G2 still does nothing to surface or route in-market accounts, and the open-pipeline slip ($3.17M → $3.01M) is Salesforce deal aging, not a G2 signal. The single highest-leverage move remains enabling the intent tier; the close second is turning this week's review burst into a sustained monthly cadence before it stalls. Until intent is on, sales/ABM should treat G2 as a positioning and objection-handling source (lead with True-SDK/no-iFrame + flat pricing against Tableau/Sisense/Looker), not a lead source — and there is no G2-attributable pipeline to action this week.
