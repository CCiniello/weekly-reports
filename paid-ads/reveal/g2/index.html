Verdict

G2 is still a proof asset, not a demand engine — because the demand half is switched off. Reveal keeps the best rating in its category and glowing switched-from-Tableau reviews, but with intent gated (retested, still no access) and review velocity at zero for seven months, G2 is doing nothing to surface or route in-market accounts. The only movement this week — open Reveal pipeline slipping $3.29M → $3.17M — is Salesforce deal aging, not a G2 signal. The single highest-leverage move is enabling the intent tier on Reveal; the second is restarting reviews to close the volume gap. Until the first happens, sales/ABM should treat G2 as a positioning and objection-handling source (lead with True-SDK/no-iFrame + flat pricing against Tableau/Sisense/Looker), not a lead source — and there is no G2-attributable pipeline to action this week.
