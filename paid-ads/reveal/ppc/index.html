Read me first. Plain-English glossary for the Reveal paid-media report — for the dev team wiring these flows into Slingshot. Anything tagged Ours is a Reveal-specific definition (how we count it), not a generic industry term.

1 · What our numbers mean

Total leads Oursgoal 250 / mo
Every distinct person who converted, across all channels. From Salesforce Lead_Event__c where ProductFamilyName__c = 'Reveal', deduped by email. The headline number because paid influences all of it.
Tier-1 quality lead Oursgoal 200 / mo
High-intent subset: a demo request, pricing quote, or gated demo, excluding spam-disqualified. Real buying-signal leads, not newsletter sign-ups.
Tier-1 quality — paid Oursgoal 100 / mo
Tier-1 quality filtered to paid (TrackingMedium__c = 'cpc'). Paid advertising's direct contribution.
Opportunity / pipeline
An opp is a real sales deal; pipeline $ sums open opp amounts. Paid pipeline = opps carrying a Google click ID (gclid).
CPL Ourstarget ≤ $75
Paid spend ÷ paid quality leads. The ≤$75 target tracks form-fills; a qualified Salesforce lead is far higher — hence the "define" status while we agree one number.
Show-up Ours
Impression share on our non-brand head terms — how often we appear when someone searches them. "Top" = above the results.

2 · Visibility & the auction

Impression share (IS)
% of available auctions where our ad showed. Top IS counts only top-of-page slots.
Lost IS — budget vs rank
The two reasons we didn't show: budget (out of money → add budget) or rank (bid/quality too low → improve those). This week everything is rank-lost.
Quality Score (QS)
Google's 1–10 grade of ad + keyword + landing-page fit. Higher = cheaper + higher rank. Ad Rank = bid × quality.
CPC / CTR / CPM
Cost per click / click-through rate / cost per 1,000 impressions.

3 · Campaign types

Search / Demand Gen / PMax
Search = text ads on queries (highest intent). Demand Gen = image/video across Google feeds (discovery). PMax = automated across all inventory.
RSA / remarketing / negatives
RSA = responsive search ad (Google mixes headlines). Remarketing = ads to prior visitors. Negatives = keywords we block to stop junk.
Smart Bidding / Max Conversions
Google sets bids automatically — you can't hand-set bids, so the lever is Quality Score, not manual bids.

4 · Keywords & targeting

Brand / non-brand / head
Brand = our name (reveal bi). Non-brand = category terms (embedded analytics). Head = the biggest non-brand terms.
Conquest / ICP / landing page
Conquest = bidding on competitor terms. ICP = ideal customer profile (SaaS/ISV, CTO/dev). Landing page = where the ad sends the click; must match the keyword or QS drops.

5 · Funnel & measurement

Engagement rate / frequency
Engagement rate (GA4) = share of sessions that were engaged. Frequency = average times one person saw the ad (impressions ÷ reach).
First vs last touch · 30-day lag Ours
First = how they discovered us; last = the click before converting. Reveal buyers research 30+ days, so discovery and conversion are weeks apart — don't cut capture for zero same-week leads.
