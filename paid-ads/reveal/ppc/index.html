The story of the week: the machine is spending correctly but the lead meter reads low — and it's a measurement problem as much as a demand problem. Spend held flat at ~$10.5k with real structural gains: Non-Brand-US impression share more than doubled (26% → 65%) as last week's rank fixes landed, brand defense climbed 58% → 62%, and the Bing spam sweep held (none of last week's junk terms recurred). But Salesforce logged only 6 paid leads vs 17 the week prior, and Google's own conversion tracking shows just 2 — because the July 16 GA4/goals re-import reset conversion memory and is still inside its 30-day relearning window. Do not read the 6 as a demand collapse: 90-day pipeline from paid stands at $382k across 9 opportunities, and Reveal's buying cycle lags 30+ days, so this week's clicks convert next month.

The two things actually holding leads down are structural and already owned: (1) ~50% of Google spend sits in Demand Gen + PMAX producing near-zero tracked conversions, and (2) paid clicks still dump onto the homepage (1,009 of ~1,300 paid sessions, 33% engagement) instead of the exact-match pages that engage 2x better. Both are in-flight. This week's net-new work is narrow: a fresh Bing junk cluster to negative out (~$90/wk), a 97%-rank-lost Bing ad group to fix, and verification that the brand + Power BI bid changes actually landed.

Break point = the landing step. Clicks are healthy and sessions arrive, but engagement collapses because ~78% of them hit the homepage (33% engagement, §4c). That's message/destination mismatch, not an offer or form problem — the pages that get the right destination convert (/request-demo drove the tracked conversions).

First-touch vs last-touch + lag: Reveal is a 30+ day research purchase. Discovery happens on a non-brand embedded-BI/competitor search; the conversion lands weeks later on brand or direct. This week's brand campaign took last-click credit while non-brand capture did the discovery. Do not judge capture on same-week leads or cut it for zero same-week conversions — the $382k/9-opp pipeline is the lagged payoff of prior capture. The soft "6 leads" is a timing and tracking artifact (7/16 conversion reset), not a demand signal.
