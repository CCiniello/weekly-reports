4e · Quality Score — the standing unlock

Standing diagnosis (keyword-level, unchanged until the two pages ship): across every Non-Brand-US ad group, landing-page experience is "Below average" on 100% of keywords and predicted CTR is "Below average" almost everywhere, while ad relevance is mostly "Above average." The ads are on-target; the pages and copy hooks pull ad rank down. Because Google won't serve a low-QS ad even when we'll pay, this is why the flagship non-brand budget can't convert into real coverage — and why this week's spike went to junk broad terms instead of head terms.

Landing-page experience — the fixes (bigger lever)
  • Build the two missing pages (/embedded-bi, /embedded-dashboards) — those ad groups have no dedicated page, so QS is capped until they exist. (Task open, due Aug 5.)
  • Repoint every ad to its exact-match page (not the homepage) and mirror the keyword in the H1.
  • Improve the existing pages (/embedded-analytics, /white-label-analytics): speed & Core Web Vitals, mobile, clear above-the-fold value, visible demo/CTA.
Predicted CTR — the second lever
  • Refresh the ad copy (RSA): pin the exact keyword into headlines, sharpen CTAs, add sitelinks/callouts (monthly copy refresh, §11).
  • Leave ad relevance alone — already "Above average"; and don't raise bids blindly, because the constraint is Quality Score, not bid.

11 Scaling — how do we grow quality volume?

Can we 10x paid volume? Not on paid search alone. This is a high-ticket, niche category; total high-intent search volume caps pure capture. The realistic near-term prize is ~1.5-2x quality leads over a quarter by unlocking what's already wasted, then adding surfaces. 10x is a portfolio question — new channels plus more mid-funnel — not a bid change.

1
Stop the Non-Brand-US broad-match leak. ~$2k/wk going to $300-525/click junk terms. Free efficiency, no media cost — the biggest immediate lever.
2
