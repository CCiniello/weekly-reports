Paid-media cheat sheet — plain English

1 · Our numbers
  • Total leads Ours — everyone who raised a hand (dedup by email), all channels. Goal 250/mo.
  • Tier-1 quality Ours — leads that asked for a demo, pricing, or gated content and aren't spam. Goal 200/mo.
  • Tier-1 paid Ours — Tier-1 leads whose tracking medium = cpc (paid ads). Goal 100/mo.
  • Quality vs raw — raw = any lead; quality = the demo/pricing/gated subset that sales actually wants.
  • Opportunity / pipeline — an opp is a real sales deal; pipeline $ is the sum of open opp amounts. Paid pipeline = opps with a Google click ID.
  • CPL Ours — paid spend ÷ paid quality leads. Target ≤$75 is under review — unrealistic at $44k/mo, hence the "define" status.
  • Show-up Ours — Top impression share on our non-brand head terms; how often we appear up top when someone searches them.
2 · Visibility & the auction
  • Impression share (IS) — % of available auctions where our ad showed.
  • Top IS — IS but only counting top-of-page slots (above results).
  • Lost IS — budget vs rank — the two reasons we didn't show: budget (out of money → add budget) or rank (bid/quality too low → improve those). This week everything is rank-lost.
  • Quality Score (QS) — Google's 1–10 grade of ad + keyword + landing-page fit; higher = cheaper + higher rank.
  • Ad Rank — bid × quality; decides position.
  • CPC / CTR / CPM — cost per click / click-through rate / cost per 1,000 impressions.
3 · Campaign types
  • Search — text ads on query results; highest intent.
  • Demand Gen — image/video across Google feeds; top-funnel discovery.
  • PMax — automated across all Google inventory; hard to see inside.
  • RSA — responsive search ad; Google mixes your headlines/descriptions.
  • Remarketing — ads to people who already visited.
  • Smart Bidding / Max Conversions — Google sets bids automatically; you can't hand-set bids, so the lever is Quality Score, not manual bids.
  • Negatives — keywords we block to stop junk/wrong-intent clicks.
4 · Keywords & targeting
  • Brand — searches for our name (reveal bi). Non-brand — category terms (embedded analytics). Head — the biggest non-brand terms.
  • Conquest — bidding on competitor terms (power bi embedded, tableau alternative).
  • ICP — ideal customer profile (SaaS/ISV embedding analytics; CTO/dev buyer).
  • Landing page — where the ad sends the click; must match the keyword or QS drops.
5 · Funnel & measurement
  • Engagement rate (GA4) — share of sessions that were "engaged"; replaces old bounce rate.
  • Frequency — average times one person saw the ad (impressions ÷ reach).
  • First vs last touch — first = how they discovered us; last = the click before converting.
  • 30-day lag Ours — Reveal buyers research 30+ days, so discovery and conversion are weeks apart — don't cut capture for zero same-week leads.
  • WoW / MTD — week-over-week / month-to-date.
