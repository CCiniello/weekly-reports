Week of Jul 25, 2026
1Executive summary
Bing is our newest and smallest paid channel for Reveal — and this week it turned a corner. For the same weekly budget as last week, it delivered meaningfully more traffic at a lower cost, and for the first time since we restarted it, that traffic began to convert. Just as important, it's buying the right audience: people actively comparing Reveal against Power BI, Tableau and Logi Analytics, who then spend real time on our comparison pages instead of bouncing.

The honest caveat is measurement. Our systems don't yet agree on how many leads Bing produced — the ad platform sees one, Google Analytics sees four, and Salesforce can't confirm any because Bing isn't set up as a lead source. So the signal is encouraging but not yet bankable.

Recommendation: keep Bing on and let it keep proving itself, but hold spend flat until we can trust the conversion numbers. It's too early to invest more and too promising to cut. The highest-value move this week isn't a bigger budget — it's fixing the tracking so next week's report can tell you, with confidence, exactly how much pipeline Bing is creating.

4What's working / what's not
Workingefficiency + engagement
More clicks for the same money — CPC fell 22% while clicks rose 31%. The traffic is high-quality: 73% engagement rate and ~3 minutes on page, landing on competitor-comparison pages that are clearly doing their job. Brand search finally got funded and produced the week's one confirmed conversion.
Not workingattribution + concentration
We can't trust the conversion count — pixel, GA4 and Salesforce all disagree. One campaign (Compete-US) is 70% of spend with no pixel conversions of its own. Bing's close-variant keyword expansion is leaking budget to off-target searches, and we're ceding 21% of our own brand impressions to rank.

1How to read this
Every Reveal Bing ad is a Responsive Search Ad (RSA) — Bing assembles headlines and descriptions on the fly. The connector reports performance per ad (impressions, CTR, conversions) but does not expose the headline/description text, so ads are identified by their Ad ID. To review the literal wording, open these Ad IDs in the Microsoft Ads UI. What we can answer here: which ads Bing is showing most, and which are earning the clicks.

Verdict — Fix

Keep the channel on, but fix before you scale. The fundamentals moved the right way — CPC down 22%, clicks up 31%, engagement up to 73%, first conversions appearing on the Tableau comparison page and home. The blockers aren't media, they're measurement and concentration: (1) close the tracking gap — reconcile the Bing pixel (1) against GA4 (4) and stand up a Bing LeadSource in Salesforce so we see pipeline, not guesses; (2) de-risk Compete-US (70% of spend) — hold flat until tracking is trustworthy; (3) fix the creative rotation — pause the two lagging Compete-US ads and feed delivery to the 2.5%+ CTR winners, and add a second Brand RSA; (4) add the four negatives to stop close-variant drift; (5) lift Brand IS from 79% toward 100% — cheap, defensive, and where the only confirmed conversion came from. Do not scale spend until we can answer "how many Bing leads reached Salesforce."
