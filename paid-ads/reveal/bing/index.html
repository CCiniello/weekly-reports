1Executive summary

Is Bing working? On raw efficiency, yes — this was the strongest top-of-funnel week in over a month. We paid $0.84 per click (down from $1.69), pulled 77% more clicks (736 vs 416), and the people who landed stuck around: engagement hit 78%, the highest in five weeks, on the lowest bounce.

Is it worth the money? Strip away the vanity and the week is thin. Not a single Bing click became a tracked GA4 conversion, versus four the week before. The cheap volume came from India and mobile — the India compete campaign alone spent $159 on 383 clicks and produced nothing — while US desktop sessions, the only segment that has ever converted here, were cut in half (241 → 121). We traded quality traffic for cheap traffic.

Recommendation: FIX, don't scale. Pull budget off India and broad close-variant expansion, push it back to US desktop embedded-analytics intent, and get conversion tracking reconciled — the Bing pixel (2) and GA4 (0) disagree and Salesforce can't see Bing at all, so we cannot honestly claim pipeline. Hold spend flat until the lead number is real.

1Budget pacing
Every Reveal campaign posted 0% impressions lost to budget this week — nothing is capped by spend. All lost impression share is lost to rank, which means the visibility levers are bid, Quality Score and ad relevance, not more money. The week's story is a reallocation, not a budget increase: spend moved off Compete-US ($490→$69) and into Compete-Core-Terms-US ($26→$230) and Compete-India ($4→$159).

Verdict — Fix

Bing bought cheaper, more engaged traffic this week but stopped converting — the cause is a budget shift into India + mobile that halved the US desktop base every prior lead has come from, compounded by tracking we can't yet trust. Hold spend flat and fix the mix before scaling. This week's highest-leverage actions:

  1. Cut the India compete budget to near-zero (or exact-only, US-HQ intent) and reallocate the ~$159/wk to Compete-US. Why: 383 clicks, 0 conversions, and it's diluting the converting desktop base. Owner: David.
  2. Rebuild the US desktop base. Push freed budget and a desktop bid modifier back into Compete-US and Core-Terms-US. Why: desktop is the only segment that converts and it halved this week. Owner: David.
  3. Add negatives & tighten close variants: private-equity, claudeai, forcebi, code-snippet patterns; route French/EMEA terms to the International campaign. Why: close-variant expansion is the leak. Owner: David.
  4. Reconcile conversion tracking. Align the Bing pixel with GA4 key events and stand up a Bing LeadSource/UTM in Salesforce. Why: pixel=2, GA4=0, SF=none is not a number we can report. Owner: Casey + analytics.
  5. Fix brand defense & add a 2nd Brand RSA. Brand IS is 75% (25% lost to rank) on a single ad. Why: we should never cede brand terms. Owner: David.
