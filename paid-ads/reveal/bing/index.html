1Executive summary

Bing is working better this week than it has all month — but it is still not paying for itself on trackable leads. Spend held flat at ~$700 while clicks jumped 31% and cost-per-click fell to $1.69, its lowest in the trend. The competitor-conquest engine (Compete-US) is doing the heavy lifting: it drove 360 of 416 clicks and pushed site engagement back up to 73% after a soft prior week.

Is it worth the money? On the numbers we can trust, not yet. Bing's own pixel logged just one conversion; GA4 attributes four key events to bing/cpc, all from desktop and all net-new visitors landing on the vs-competitor pages. Even taking the generous GA4 count, cost-per-lead is ~$176 — well above our $75 ceiling — and we still cannot confirm a single Bing lead in Salesforce because there is no clean Bing LeadSource.

Recommendation: HOLD and fix, do not scale. The channel is finally cheap and engaging, so this is the week to fix tracking (UTM casing + SF attribution), plug the close-variant spend leaks, and give the brand campaign a second RSA and a rank bump. Prove a real lead lands in the CRM before adding budget.

4Working / not working
What's workingmomentum
CPC fell to $1.69 (−22%) while clicks rose +31% — more traffic for the same money. Engagement rebounded to 73% and bounce dropped to 27%. The vs-competitor pages convert: /reveal-vs-tableau and the homepage produced all 4 GA4 events, and the one brand query (“reveal dashboard”) fired the Bing pixel. Desktop + net-new visitors carry the quality.
What's not workingleaks & blind spots
Tracking is not trustworthy: pixel says 1, GA4 says 4, Salesforce says nothing. A CPC-vs-cpc UTM split is scattering sessions. Close-variant expansion is leaking spend — ~$36 on “who is bi worldwide's biggest competitor” and $8 on “grok build competitors,” both off-ICP. Non-Brand-US burned $51 for 2 clicks ($25 CPC). Brand loses 21% of impressions to rank.
1Budget pacing

No campaign is capped by budget this week — every campaign shows 0% impressions lost to budget. The visibility we're missing is a rank problem, not a spend problem, so more budget is not the lever right now. Compete-US absorbs the majority of spend ($490 of $703) and still holds 83% impression share. The three newly launched campaigns (Compete-Core-Terms-US, Compete-India, Non-Brand-India) are in learning and should be judged on a 4-week horizon, not today.

Broad match is not the leak — the account is essentially 100% Exact bidded. The leak vector on Bing is “Exact (close variant)” expansion: Microsoft delivers Exact keywords against loosely-related queries. 3The Salesforce attribution gap
We cannot confirm Bing pipeline in Salesforce. There is no clean Bing LeadSource, so lead/opportunity attribution for this channel is traced only through GA4 bing / cpc + UTM — not the CRM. This week that trace shows 4 GA4 key events vs 1 Bing pixel conversion; the gap is definitional (GA4 counts multiple key-event types per session, the Bing pixel counts one primary action). Until a Bing LeadSource exists, treat all Bing “conversion” counts as engagement proxies, not confirmed leads, and do not scale budget on them.
Verdict — Hold & Fix (do not scale)

Bing got cheaper and more engaging this week — $1.69 CPC, +31% clicks, 73% engagement — but it still can't prove a $75 lead, and tracking is too broken to trust a scale decision. Fix the plumbing and stop the leaks this week; revisit budget only once a real Bing lead lands in Salesforce.
