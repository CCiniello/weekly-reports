A healthy operating week with one clear structural signal: visibility jumped without spending more. Search impression share climbed from 64% to 75% and budget-lost share collapsed to near zero (0.3%) — meaning budget is no longer the constraint. Where we still lose impressions, we lose them to Ad Rank, not money. That reframes the whole growth question: throwing dollars at the US head terms won't move the needle; bids, Quality Score and ad relevance will.

What's working best: the demand engine is efficient — CPC fell 14% to $3.51 and clicks rose 16% on flat spend. Brand and compete search remain the highest-quality traffic on site (Brand engagement 56%, 6+ min sessions). International capture (India, Brazil) is dominating its auctions at 87–93% IS.

Where to focus: three things. (1) Brand defense is leaking — we hold only 62% IS on our own name, losing 38% to rank. (2) 85% of paid clicks land on the homepage, including high-intent compete and non-brand searches that should hit dedicated pages. (3) Lead capture is under-instrumented — the Google Ads conversion import is still not firing (column reads 0), so we are flying on Salesforce Lead Events and GA4 alone. Fix the import before trusting any CPL number.

Verdict — this week

KEEP & SCALE. The engine is efficient and visibility is climbing on flat spend. The story of the week is that budget is no longer the constraint — rank is — so the growth levers are quality/bid, not dollars.

Priorities (highest leverage first):

1. Reclaim brand defense — we hold only 62% IS on our own name (38% lost to rank). Raise brand bids + tighten brand ad relevance to get back toward 100%. Cheapest, highest-return fix.
2. Route traffic off the homepage — 85% of paid clicks hit /. Point compete → /reveal-vs-*, non-brand → /embedded-analytics. The vs-pages already show the lowest bounce.
3. Fix the conversion import + broken UTMs — until Google can optimize on real leads, we're under-instrumented and CPL is unmeasurable.
4. Lift US non-brand & compete IS via rank (not budget) — improve QS/ad relevance on /embedded-analytics and vs-pages; scale the underserved high-CTR proof/compete copy.
5. Add the SAP / QuickSight / Looker exact keywords — confirmed gaps, commercial intent, low difficulty.
