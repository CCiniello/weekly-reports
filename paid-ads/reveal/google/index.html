1How to read this
Reveal plays in an expensive, high-intent space — so “costs a lot per click” is not a reason to cut. The rule: if a query contains embedded analytics, embedded BI, white-label, OEM analytics, or a competitor + embed/pricing/alternative, it’s on-strategy and we keep it even at a high CPC. We only trim genuinely broad or junk queries. This tab does two things: flags the small number of real wasters, and — more useful — surfaces high-intent queries to add as exact match (pulled from our own search terms + Ahrefs). We are not recommending phrase match; it brought noise last time.

Verdict — KEEP & optimize

Paid is now provably tied to pipeline — ~$821K influenced across 20 Google/PPC opportunities this year, led by branded and competitor search — so this is a keep-and-optimize, not a cut. The economics are moving the right way (CPC −14%, clicks +19% at flat spend).

Three moves this week: (1) Investigate the lead dip — 20 → 7 paid leads WoW at flat spend; check form/offer and confirm it’s noise, not a break. (2) Defend brand — 57.6% IS on our own name at ~$24 CPC points to a competitor bidding on reveal bi; raise brand bids/QS and check the SERP. (3) Expand high-intent coverage — add the exact-match embedded-analytics / competitor-embed terms on the Search tab, and shift a slice of DemandGen homepage budget toward the vs-pages and pricing page that actually convert.
