VP Readout — 30-second view
Goal: qualified demand on the terms buyers search — efficient CPL & pipeline
Fix

One Non-Brand US search click cost $1,049 — the week's biggest leak

Non-Brand-US search spend jumped 6× to $2,334 for 10 clicks (a single "embeddable analytics" broad-ish click ran $1,049). Tighten to exact match + add negatives; that one campaign drove most of the +20% spend and the CPC spike to $4.88.

Working

Branded search is the efficient engine — 3 leads at ~$86 each

Brand terms delivered 3 of 7 paid leads for just $259, right at the $75 CPL target, and branded search + PMax remarketing are what's producing the $40–50K pipeline opps (e.g. Broadridge, $47.5K).

Watch

Visibility is lost to rank, not budget — and brand defense slipped to 57%

Every point of lost impression share is rank-driven (budget-lost IS = 0), so more money won't help; bids/quality will. Brand IS fell to 57% (43% lost to rank) — competitors are outranking us on our own name.

Executive summary

A steady-volume week that got more expensive without getting more productive. Impressions held flat (~207K) but clicks fell 14% while spend rose 20%, pushing blended CPC from $3.51 to $4.88 — almost entirely because the Non-Brand US search campaign absorbed a handful of very expensive broad clicks. Underneath the noise, the engine that matters is healthy: branded search again converted efficiently (3 leads near the $75 CPL target) and paid-sourced pipeline continues to skew toward branded search and PMax remarketing, with a fresh $47.5K New Logo opp (Broadridge) attributed to paid. Paid leads held flat at 7 for the week — a ~30/mo run-rate from Google, one channel of the 100/mo all-paid floor.

Where to focus: this is a discipline week, not a budget week. Budget-lost impression share is zero everywhere, so all visibility gaps are rank/quality problems — including a brand-defense slip to 57% IS. The fastest wins are tightening Non-Brand US match types, raising brand bids to reclaim our own name, and routing paid traffic off the homepage (84% of paid sessions land on "/") toward the intent and comparison pages that actually engage.

Note: the Google Ads in-platform conversion column is unreliable (offline import not firing) and is not used here — lead truth comes from Salesforce Lead Event, pipeline from Slingshot. GA4 sessions for the last 1–2 days are still maturing (~48h lag).

Verdict — Keep & Fix

The paid engine is fundamentally sound and pipeline-positive — brand + PMax remarketing are producing $40–50K opps — but this week it got 20% more expensive for 14% fewer clicks, almost entirely from one undisciplined US non-brand search campaign. This is a tightening week, not a scaling week. No budget increase is warranted: all lost visibility is rank-driven.
