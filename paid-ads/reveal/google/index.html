Do not scale and do not cut on this week’s numbers — they can’t be trusted downstream. But with 0 Google Ads conversions, 1 GA4 key event, and 0 SF-attributed leads, the #1 action is to restore conversion tracking and Salesforce LeadSource attribution this week — nothing else can be judged until it’s live. In parallel, two no-regret fixes: (1) defend brand — 57.6% IS at $24 CPC means we’re losing our own name to rank; raise brand bids/QS and check for a competitor on reveal bi; and (2) reallocate within Search — trim high-CPC single-click research terms and push saved budget toward the pages that already engage (/reveal-vs-power-bi, /pricing/embedded-analytics).
