1 The read for leadership

Are we getting ROI, and is the channel healthy? Same honest answer as last week, now with a stronger week behind it: LinkedIn is buying us high-quality attention from exactly the right people, and it's getting more efficient — but we still can't prove it turns into pipeline. Closing that measurement gap remains the whole job.

What we're achieving For about $1,240 this week we put Reveal in front of ~4,200 senior decision-makers at mid-market tech companies — founders, CEOs, CTOs, and VPs of Engineering and Product. The engine accelerated: clicks jumped 23% to 1,168 while CPC fell to $1.06 (down 7%) and CTR climbed to 5.12%. Audience quality held — ~87% of impressions hit leadership and senior tiers, with Engineering share rising. The top of the funnel is not just healthy, it improved week over week.

Where we can't claim ROI yet — and I'll stay straight about it We still cannot connect a dollar of this spend to a lead or opportunity. Two reasons, unchanged: no lead-capture form is running, so LinkedIn hands us zero leads; and our ad links carry no tracking, so of 1,168 clicks this week our analytics saw about 10 sessions — and only one was tagged as LinkedIn paid. Any pipeline this influences is being credited to other channels. The performance is almost certainly real; we just can't measure it, which for a budget decision is the same as not having it.

The call Fix it, don't cut it. A second strong week has earned the channel the right to be measured properly. Turn on link tracking and a lead form, trim ~$450/week of clearly wasted spend (a starved retargeting campaign running expired event creative), and within two weeks I can put a real cost-per-lead and ROI figure in front of you.

3 Messaging & ad copy — what to keep, what to refresh
Keep — engineer-opportunity-cost4.6–9.95% CTR
Copy: "'Can we get analytics in the app?' — your customers. Building it yourself means quarters of eng time and a maintenance burden that never ends."
Why it works: problem-first, ICP-specific, and speaks to the VP Eng / product-leader tension of protecting roadmap time. This hook powers both the top image ad and the top video — our sharpest, most repeatable angle.
Keep — build-vs-buy cost/risk5.3% CTR
Copy: "Building embedded analytics in-house now runs into a multi-year, six-figure commitment — and the costs that actually kill you (tenancy, AI, compliance, maintenance) never show up in the prototype."
Why it works: it names the exact hidden costs a CTO fears — multi-tenancy, AI grounding, compliance — so it reads as insider truth, not a pitch. Refresh: remove "See the real numbers, July 21"; point to an evergreen cost breakdown or ROI calculator.
Refresh — event & demo copy0.4–0.8% CTR
Copy: "Your seat is still open — July 21" and "You've seen what Reveal can do. Now see it running…"
Why it fails: the first is an expired deadline; the second assumes prior awareness we don't have with a cold audience. Refresh: retire the event CTAs, and for cold traffic swap the demo ask for a lower-friction offer (the cost-breakdown asset or a 15-min product tour).
Add — CTO proof pointopportunity
Gap: CXO and Senior tiers are among our highest-CTR titles (5.5–6.1%), yet no single ad leads on the hardest technical objections — security, multi-tenant data isolation, compliance. Why it matters: a proof-led unit (e.g., "SOC 2, row-level multi-tenant security, out of the box") could convert our most valuable, most engaged segment. Worth a test.

Verdict

FIX — do not scale yet, do not cut. Two strong weeks now: efficient and improving CPC/CTR (clicks +23%, CPC –7%) and a genuinely on-ICP audience (founders, CTOs, VP Eng/Product at mid-market tech), with Engineering and VP share tightening further. But the channel is still operationally blind — broken UTM tracking hides ~99% of clicks from GA4 and Salesforce, no lead-gen form exists, and ~$450/wk leaks into a starved retargeting campaign running expired creative. Fix tracking (UTMs + a lead form) and kill the waste over the next 1–2 weeks, then re-evaluate for scale with real cost-per-lead in hand. Cutting now would kill a channel that's working before we've ever measured it; scaling now would just buy more invisible clicks.
