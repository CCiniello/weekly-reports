1 Executive summary
LinkedIn is doing its amplification job well but flying blind on outcomes. Spend held at $1,143 (+5%) and every efficiency metric improved WoW — clicks +17%, CTR up to 5.11% (+0.7pp), CPC down to $1.09 (–10%). Audience quality is genuinely strong: ~80% of impressions hit Owner/CXO/VP/Director seniorities and the top job titles are Founders, CEOs, CTOs and engineering leaders at mid-market tech companies — squarely Reveal's ICP.

But two things break the story. First, zero leads and zero lead-form activity — all three campaigns run WEBSITE_VISIT / WEBSITE_CONVERSION objectives, so there is no LinkedIn lead-gen form capturing anyone. Second, and more urgent: LinkedIn logged 1,051 clicks this week, yet GA4 saw only 8 LinkedIn sessions. The paid clicks are not landing with a linkedin / paid source-medium, which means GA4 — and therefore Salesforce — cannot see this channel at all. We cannot currently tie a single dollar of LinkedIn spend to pipeline. Fix tracking before scaling.

1 The GA4 story — and the hole in it
This is where the channel breaks down. LinkedIn Ads reported 1,051 clicks this week. GA4 (property 516472465) recorded just 8 LinkedIn sessions, all tagged linkedin.com / referral — there is no linkedin / paid or cpc medium in GA4 at all. The paid ad URLs are not carrying UTM parameters, so GA4 either drops the visits into "direct/unassigned" or doesn't associate them with the campaign. Effectively ~99% of paid LinkedIn traffic is invisible downstream.

4 Salesforce attribution — honest note
There is no reliable LinkedIn → Salesforce attribution path this week, and this is a known gap. Salesforce has no clean LinkedIn LeadSource, so attribution depends on (a) GA4 linkedin / paid source-medium, (b) LinkedIn lead-gen form submissions, or (c) UTM parameters flowing into SF. Right now all three are empty: no paid medium reaches GA4, no lead-gen form is running, and UTMs aren't on the ad URLs. Any pipeline LinkedIn is influencing is currently being credited to Direct, Organic, or last-touch Brand search. Do not read "0 leads / 0 opps" as "LinkedIn isn't working" — read it as "we can't see what LinkedIn is doing."

Verdict

FIX — do not scale yet, do not cut. The fundamentals are good: efficient and improving CPC/CTR, and an audience that is genuinely on-ICP (founders, CTOs, VP Eng/Product at mid-market tech). But the channel is operationally blind — broken UTM tracking hides ~99% of clicks from GA4 and Salesforce, no lead-gen form exists, and ~$450/wk leaks into expired creative, dead retargeting, and off-ICP audiences. Fix tracking (UTMs + a lead-gen form) and trim the waste over the next 1–2 weeks. Once we can actually see click → session → lead, re-evaluate for scale — the audience quality earns that second look. Cutting now would kill a channel that's working before we've ever measured it.
