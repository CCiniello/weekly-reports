# Paid Performance Report Standard

## Tabs
1. Executive Report
2. Budget & Forecast
3. Funnel & Outcomes
4. Tasks
5. Summary & Definitions

## Required distinctions
- Reveal vs UI Tools
- Search vs audience/demand
- Brand vs non-brand vs competitor
- United States vs EMEA/international
- Directly attributable vs directional/associated
- Live metric vs carried metric
- Healthy data vs measurement gap

Every budget recommendation must state: amount, from, to, reason, expected result, confidence, and dependency.
