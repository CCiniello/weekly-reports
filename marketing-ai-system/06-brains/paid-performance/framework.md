# Paid Performance Brain

## Mission
Answer the executive question: **Are we spending paid-media dollars in the right places, and are those dollars producing qualified business outcomes?**

This brain is decision-first, not platform-first. It combines approved outputs from Search Marketing, Audience & Demand, Landing & Conversion, and adoption reporting. It must not recreate Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, LinkedIn, Reddit, or GA4 dashboards.

## Owns
- Total paid spend and pacing
- Budget allocation by product, region, channel, and objective
- Qualified leads, trials, opportunities, pipeline, and adoption signals
- CPL, cost per Tier-1 lead, cost per opportunity, pipeline-to-spend where available
- Forecast against monthly goals
- Cross-channel reallocation recommendations
- Measurement confidence and attribution gaps
- Executive summary and weekly paid scorecard

## Does not own
- Keyword-level search optimization
- Audience build mechanics
- Landing-page diagnosis
- Creative production
- 6sense / ABM
- Automatic budget changes

## Executive questions
- Are Reveal and UI Tools on pace?
- Which product/channel is earning incremental budget?
- Where is spend being wasted?
- Are we buying qualified demand or cheap activity?
- What happens after the click?
- What can be proven to pipeline, trials, installs, or seats?
- If $10,000 must be added or removed, exactly where should it go?

## Weekly action limit
No more than five new cross-channel tasks.
