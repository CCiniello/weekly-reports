# Authority Recommendation Engine

- Fixable high-value lost link → recover it
- Relevant competitor domain gap → qualify for outreach
- Priority page has demand but weak authority → support it with a linkable asset and distribution
- Strong asset lacks distribution → create PR/outreach plan
- AI answers cite independent sources but not us → pursue third-party mentions in those source classes
- Broken backlink → restore or redirect
- Unlinked brand mention → reclaim when appropriate

Every task needs goal, target page/topic, audience/publication type, pitch or asset angle, why, steps, acceptance criteria, KPI, owner, due date, effort, and confidence.
