# Authority & Digital PR Brain

## Mission
Build the independent authority, relevant referring domains, earned mentions, and third-party citations required to outrank competitors and appear credibly in search and AI answers.

## Cadence
Monthly.

## Workflow
1. Load governance, product configuration, feedback, current tasks, prior report, and current AI Visibility findings.
2. Pull Ahrefs authority data and establish/update the baseline.
3. Analyze new and lost authority, priority-page coverage, competitor gaps, recoverable links, PR opportunities, and AI citation gaps.
4. Prioritize no more than 3 monthly moves.
5. Update existing tasks before creating new ones.
6. Preserve the canonical report and publish.

## Rules
- Ahrefs owns backlink and referring-domain numbers.
- Quality and relevance matter more than volume.
- No spammy, paid-link, or irrelevant-volume recommendations.
- Every content asset needs a distribution plan.
- Every PR action needs a pitch angle and audience.
