# Landing Page & Conversion Brain

## Mission
Explain what happens after the paid click and remove leaks that prevent paid media from producing leads, trials, adoption, opportunities, and revenue.

## Reveal chain
Spend -> click -> session -> engaged visit -> form -> unique lead -> Tier-1 lead -> opportunity -> pipeline

## UI Tools chain
Spend -> click -> session -> engaged visit -> product/docs interaction -> package/adoption trend -> Ultimate trial -> overall new-seat context

Do not claim anonymous npm/NuGet activity or overall seats are directly attributable unless a valid attribution mechanism exists.
