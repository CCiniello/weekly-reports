# Market Presence Task Standard

## Limits

- Maximum 5 new tasks per run
- Maximum 3 weekly priorities
- Update existing tasks before creating new ones
- Do not create one task per placement or person when a grouped action is more efficient

## Task types

- Placement
- Community
- Influencer
- Relationship
- Technical

## Required task content

- Action-led title
- Track
- Why it matters
- Evidence
- Step-by-step action
- Acceptance criteria
- Success KPI
- Owner
- Start date
- Due date
- Effort
- Opportunity score
- Links
- Approval required: Yes / No

## Default timing

- Listing correction or claim: 7 business days
- Placement submission package: 10 business days
- Community response draft: 2 business days
- Community content plan: 7 business days
- Influencer qualification or outreach draft: 5 business days
- Collaboration brief: 10 business days
- Technical listing/site fix: 5–10 business days

## Write safety

If the Slingshot list's task type or accepted write parameters cannot be resolved:
- Do not invent them
- Publish proposed tasks in the report
- Log the blocker
- Continue the remaining safe workflow
