# Relationship Pipeline Standard

The report and task system must maintain a lightweight marketing relationship CRM.

## Core fields

- Person or organization
- Type: Placement / Community / Influencer / Partner / Media
- Product
- Relationship stage
- Owner
- Last touch date
- Next action date
- Opportunity
- Evidence/source
- Notes
- Estimated impact
- Status
- Opportunity score

## Pipeline health goals

- Every active relationship has an owner
- Every active relationship has a next step
- No active relationship is untouched for more than 30 days without a documented reason
- No duplicate person or organization records
- Closed or not-fit opportunities retain the reason
- Completed collaborations record the outcome and reusable learning

## Weekly maintenance

- Advance stages only with evidence
- Flag overdue follow-ups
- Surface warm introductions
- Archive low-fit opportunities
- Carry forward only the highest-value active work
