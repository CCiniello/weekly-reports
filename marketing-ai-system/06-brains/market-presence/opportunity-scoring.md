# Market Presence Opportunity Scoring

Score each opportunity from 1–5.

Weighted score:
- ICP relevance: 25%
- Buyer trust / influence: 20%
- Reach or audience quality: 15%
- Competitive gap: 15%
- Likely business or adoption impact: 15%
- Effort and feasibility: 10%

For effort and feasibility, 5 means easy and low dependency.

## Priority rules

High priority:
- Strong ICP concentration
- Competitors are present and we are absent
- Trusted source or influential person
- Clear next step
- Reasonable effort
- No duplicate work

Medium priority:
- Valuable but needs validation, relationship warming, or an asset first

Low priority:
- Broad audience with weak ICP fit
- Pay-to-play with unclear value
- Spammy directory or low-quality community
- No practical next step

Only High-confidence, High-priority opportunities may become automatic tasks.
