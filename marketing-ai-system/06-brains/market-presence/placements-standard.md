# Placements Standard

## Goal

Be present, accurate, and competitive everywhere buyers reasonably expect to discover or validate the product.

## Placement types

- Review and comparison sites
- Cloud, software, developer, and partner marketplaces
- Technology and integration directories
- Vendor and ecosystem partner pages
- Product discovery platforms
- Conference, sponsor, speaker, and event directories
- Industry resource lists
- Agency and consultant directories
- Developer-tool and component-library indexes
- Template, extension, and integration galleries

## Required fields

- Placement name
- URL
- Type
- Product
- Status: Missing / Claimed / Live / Needs Update / Submitted / Pending / Rejected
- Competitors present
- ICP relevance
- Evidence
- Owner
- Last checked
- Next step
- Opportunity score

## Rules

- Never recommend a placement solely for a backlink.
- Do not submit to low-quality, irrelevant, or spammy directories.
- Flag paid placements separately and require human approval before spend.
- Check existing listings before creating a submission task.
- Group related listing updates into one task when practical.
