# Community Standard

## Goal

Become a consistently useful and credible participant in the communities where buyers, developers, and practitioners solve relevant problems.

## Community types

- Reddit
- Stack Overflow and Stack Exchange
- GitHub Discussions and Issues
- DEV, Hashnode, and technical publishing communities
- Slack and Discord groups
- Microsoft, Power BI, Fabric, React, Angular, Blazor, analytics, product, project-management, and marketing communities
- Professional associations and practitioner forums

## Opportunity types

- Unanswered high-value question
- Brand or competitor mention
- Product misconception
- Feature request or pain signal
- Relevant discussion worth monitoring
- Community content opportunity
- Expert participation opportunity
- Community partnership or AMA

## Safety and quality

- Do not auto-post.
- Do not astroturf, hide affiliation, or impersonate a customer.
- Do not recommend promotional replies where a useful answer is not possible.
- Draft responses must disclose affiliation when appropriate.
- Community value and credibility outrank link acquisition.
- Capture recurring pain points and language for Product Marketing and Content.
