# Market Presence Report Standard

## Canonical behavior

The first successful report becomes the canonical template for the product. Future runs must preserve its HTML, CSS, JavaScript, tabs, brand, and interactions.

Use a self-contained four-tab report:

1. Report
2. Tasks
3. Relationship Pipeline
4. Summary & Definitions

## Report tab order

1. VP Readout
2. Executive Summary
3. Shared Goal Scoreboard
4. KPI Snapshot
5. Placements
6. Community
7. Influencers
8. Competitor Presence Gaps
9. Weekly Priorities
10. Forecast and Risks
11. Source and Methodology Status

## Shared Goal Scoreboard

Columns:
- Goal
- Baseline
- Current
- 30-day target
- 90-day target
- Progress
- Insight
- Status

Goals:
- Priority placement coverage
- Competitor-only placement gap
- Qualified community footprint
- Useful community opportunities addressed
- Qualified influencer relationships
- Active relationship pipeline health
- Collaborations or placements launched
- Referral or business impact where measurable

## KPI Snapshot

- Priority placements live
- Missing high-value placements
- Active communities monitored
- Qualified community opportunities
- Qualified influencers identified
- Active relationships
- Overdue follow-ups
- Collaborations launched

## Tasks tab

Render the live Market Presence task list with Table, Board, and Timeline views. Clearly show track: Placement, Community, Influencer, Relationship, or Technical.

## Relationship Pipeline tab

Show:
- Person / organization
- Type
- Stage
- Owner
- Last touch
- Next step
- Opportunity
- Score

## Summary & Definitions tab

Include:
- Leadership-ready summary with copy button
- Definitions
- Scope boundaries between Market Presence and Authority
- Source limitations
