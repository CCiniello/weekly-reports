# Influencer and Trusted-Voice Standard

## Goal

Build durable relationships with people buyers and practitioners already trust.

## Relationship types

- YouTube creators
- Newsletter writers
- Podcasters
- Analysts
- Consultants
- Microsoft MVPs and other recognized experts
- Developer advocates
- Conference speakers
- Authors
- Educators and course creators
- Community leaders

## Relationship stages

- Identified
- Qualified
- Warm introduction available
- Outreach drafted
- Contacted
- Responded
- Active relationship
- Collaboration planned
- Collaboration live
- Nurture
- Closed / Not Fit

## Required relationship fields

- Person
- Organization/channel
- Role/type
- Audience and platform
- Product/topic fit
- Relationship stage
- Owner
- Last touch
- Next step
- Opportunity
- Evidence
- Estimated value
- Risk or conflict
- Opportunity score

## Rules

- Relationship quality outranks follower count.
- Do not promise payment, sponsorship, free licenses, travel, exclusivity, or product commitments without human approval.
- Do not automate outreach.
- Prefer credible collaborative value: expert input, webinar, podcast, benchmark, review, tutorial, event, byline, or customer education.
