# Audience & Demand Brain

## Mission
Determine whether awareness and demand-generation spend is reaching the right people and accounts, with the right message and frequency, and whether it is creating measurable downstream influence.

## V1 scope
- LinkedIn Ads
- Reddit Ads
- Google Demand Gen
- Retargeting

6sense and ABM are excluded.

## Channel jobs
- LinkedIn: ICP reach, account/role quality, engagement, lead-gen quality, influence
- Reddit: developer/practitioner awareness, relevant reach, site quality, recall
- Demand Gen: prospecting/retargeting quality, creative learning, assisted conversion

## Guardrails
Do not call broad off-ICP reach successful. Do not scale low-quality activity. Do not auto-change audiences, exclusions, budgets, frequency, or creative. Do not claim pipeline influence without evidence.
