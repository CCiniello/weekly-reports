# Search Marketing Report Standard

## Tabs
1. Executive Report
2. Google + Microsoft
3. Search Terms & Keywords
4. Regions, Devices & Landing Pages
5. Journey & Outcomes
6. Tasks
7. Summary & Definitions

## Mandatory analysis
Spend allocation; impression share and loss reason; search-term intent; exact keyword gaps; negatives; brand/non-brand/competitor split; product/framework split; country; device; landing routing; outcome quality; exact budget recommendation.

Lead every section with the decision, not a dashboard dump.
