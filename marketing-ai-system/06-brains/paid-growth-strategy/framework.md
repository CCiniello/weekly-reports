# Paid Growth Strategy Brain

## Mission
Identify the highest-value paid-growth opportunities the current account structure is missing.

This brain reads the other paid reports and thinks; it does not reproduce raw channel analysis.

## Required questions
- What campaign should exist but does not?
- Which high-intent cluster is underfunded or uncovered?
- Which competitor searches deserve more investment?
- Which region or device needs a separate strategy?
- Which ad type or audience is missing?
- Which landing page suppresses ROI?
- What should receive the next $10,000?
- What should lose $10,000?
- What test produces the most learning per dollar?

Maximum five strategic moves. No automatic spend changes. No 6sense/ABM.
