The goal & where we stand

Goal: be present & cited in AI answers on the dev/component topics developers actually ask about, and grow share-of-voice vs Telerik/KendoReact, DevExpress, Syncfusion and AG Grid — week over week.

Where we stand: Ignite UI's parent brand Infragistics sits at 6.1% AI visibility, down from 7.1% last week, tied for last with Figma and roughly 5–7× behind Material UI (40.8%), Syncfusion (32.7%) and Ant Design (30.6%). Even our direct paid-suite peers DevExpress and Kendo UI (22.4% each) out-appear us ~4×. Average answer rank is 9.6 of ~10. The one positive: prompt coverage rose 50% → 75%. When we do get named, it's almost always our own comparison blogs doing the work; the neutral third-party listicles LLMs trust still leave us out.

Read this cut with two caveats (both now action items for JJ): (1) AI Visibility tracks only the parent "Infragistics" brand — there is no Ignite UI / React / Angular / Blazor brand, so nothing here isolates the product or a framework. (2) The account currently queries just 4 prompts in a single populated topic ("React – Best-of & Discovery"), where we score 0% — and AG Grid, the most-cited grid in our own source data, isn't even a tracked competitor. We are being measured on almost nothing, and losing what little we're measured on. Fixing measurement is this week's top lever.

Executive summary

AI Search Visibility / Ignite UI — week of Jul 20–26, 2026

Headline: Ignite UI's parent brand Infragistics sits at 6.1% AI visibility (down from 7.1% WoW), tied for last with Figma and 5–7× behind Material UI (40.8%), Syncfusion (32.7%) and Ant Design (30.6%). Direct paid-suite peers DevExpress and Kendo UI (22.4% each) out-appear us ~4×. Avg answer rank 9.6/10. Bright spot: prompt coverage rose 50% → 75%.

What / why

The problem is presence, not polish — competitors get named in AI answers on enterprise, data-grid and comparison prompts; we don't. When we are named, it's almost entirely our own comparison blogs (our data-grid performance guide surfaces Ignite in 28.6% of answers where infragistics.com is the source). The neutral third-party listicles LLMs trust most — Reddit, Medium, builder.io, hashbyt — omit us while listing MUI, Ant Design and AG Grid.

Two measurement gaps make this cut thinner than it looks: Amplitude tracks only the parent "Infragistics" brand (no Ignite UI / framework brands), and it queries just 4 prompts in one topic where we score 0% — with AG Grid, the most-cited grid in our data, not even a tracked competitor.

Progress & next moves

  • Fix measurement (top lever): add Ignite UI / React / Angular / Blazor brands in Amplitude; expand the prompt set to data-grid, Angular, Blazor and comparison intent; add AG Grid as a competitor.
  • Ship llms.txt — currently absent — mapping Ignite product hubs + comparison blogs.
  • Scale what works: more answer-first, schema-marked comparison pages (Angular grid, Kendo vs Ignite, AG Grid alternatives, Best Blazor).
  • Make our ranking docs AI-legible: TL;DR answer blocks + FAQ/HowTo schema on top Angular component docs.

All five actions are assigned to JJ McGuigan and dated (start Jul 27; due Aug 3–17).
