# Reveal SEO Report Standard

## Canonical artifact

The approved HTML file is the canonical structural and visual template. Every run must preserve its HTML structure, CSS, JavaScript, component hierarchy, four tabs, feedback loop, interactions, brand treatment, and responsive behavior.

The agent may change only:
- Report date and reporting windows
- Live metrics and source-status labels
- Goal values approved in the source map
- Current insights and forecast
- Tracker-rendered task data
- Approved definitions and allowlist changes
- Run-specific feedback context

The agent may not:
- Simplify markup
- Remove columns or sections
- Change design tokens
- Add or remove report sections
- Change CSS or JavaScript
- Rename headings or tabs
- Replace GT Walsheim
- change interaction behavior
- redesign because a file is large or retrieval is inconvenient

## Required executive questions

Every weekly report must answer:

1. What is the high-level business summary?
2. Are we on track for the 250-total / 50-organic lead plan?
3. Is organic lead quality high, including Tier 1 lead volume?
4. Is high-intent organic volume increasing?
5. Are organic efforts creating opportunities, and what is their status?
6. What are the top three things working?
7. What are the top three things not working?
8. What exactly are we doing about the three most important problems?
9. What is the forecast?
10. What data is missing, stale, partial, or rebaselined?

## Summary language

- Direct and outcome-led.
- No rhetorical opener.
- Short paragraphs.
- Distinguish reach, traffic, lead volume, quality, and opportunity creation.
- Do not claim causation when evidence supports only correlation.
- Do not call total organic sessions successful when the approved high-intent allowlist is behind.
- Explain whether movement is from implementation, demand, seasonality, instrumentation, or an unknown factor.

## Forecast standard

Forecast must include:
- Expected month-end organic leads
- Expected Tier 1 organic leads when available
- Expected high-intent sessions
- Probability of hitting the target: High / Medium / Low
- Main upside driver
- Main downside risk
- Assumptions
- Confidence

Use a simple transparent method and label partial-month estimates. Do not present a forecast when the underlying data is unavailable or too incomplete.

## Source status

Include a concise source-status note or run-health statement:
- Fresh
- Partial
- Stale
- Unavailable
- Rebaselined

## Task summary

State:
- New tasks created
- Existing tasks updated
- Completed tasks validated
- Proposed actions not created and why
- Blockers or owner questions
