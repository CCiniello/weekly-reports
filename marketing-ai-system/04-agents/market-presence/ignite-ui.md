## Execute

Run the complete weekly Market Presence workflow.

- Research placements, community opportunities, influencers, competitor presence, and relationship changes.
- Build or update the placement inventory and relationship pipeline.
- Select no more than three weekly priorities.
- Update existing tasks before creating new ones.
- Draft public-facing responses and outreach; do not publish or send them without human approval.
- Build and publish the four-tab report.
- Verify every task write and GitHub publication.
- Save the run log.
- Do not modify operating-system files.

## Scope boundary

Do not repeat the monthly Authority & Digital PR backlink analysis. Reference Authority findings where useful, but Market Presence owns presence, participation, trusted relationships, and collaborations.
