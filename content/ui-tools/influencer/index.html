1 VP Readout
Goal: get credible developer voices recommending Ignite UI in the places devs actually decide — YouTube, GitHub, Reddit, dev.to, and the newsletters buyers read — and grow earned mentions & AI citations.

Where we stand (first run): Ignite UI is at 0% AI share of voice on tracked component-library prompts, and no genuine earned editorial placement landed on the Ignite UI product page this window. But two things break in our favor: (1) the commercial-vendor tier — Telerik/Kendo, DevExpress, PrimeNG — is also at ~0%, so the enterprise slot in AI answers is unclaimed; and (2) Ignite UI just went open source across all four frameworks, which turns seeding and "free inclusion" pitches from a hard sell into an easy yes.

The play: stop chasing raw reach. The fastest lever is getting Ignite UI onto the AI-cited comparison surfaces (dev.to grid listicles, LogRocket, Medium roundups) that LLMs already quote, and pairing that with a small set of on-ICP creators carrying one line: don't waste tokens reinventing a data grid — generate against Ignite UI.

3 Telerik AI / MCP Gap Plan
What Telerik shipped: 2026 Q1 + Q2 releases with MCP servers for Angular, React, jQuery and ASP.NET Core; an Agentic UI Generator; a hallucination-reducing validator; and a CLI that auto-sets-up MCP servers. They currently own the "agentic UI generation" narrative.

Our counter-plan:
  • Standalone AI/MCP YouTube content — "generate a production grid against Ignite UI".
  • Stand up MCP product pages for Ignite UI (per framework).
  • Launch an AI Early Access program for creators/MVPs.
  • 48-hour newsjacking of competitor AI releases (Telerik Q2 is live now).
  • Recruit external advocates to carry the AI story — not just first-party posts.
