Jason’s core reframe

AI made the cost of no structure visible. Stop selling components as a feature list — in an agentic world, battle-tested components are the trusted substrate AI builds on: “don’t waste tokens reinventing a data grid; generate against Ignite UI.” Telerik treats AI/MCP as an ongoing editorial beat; Ignite UI treats it as a launch. Closing that gap is the whole play.
