1What changed since last week
Two real moves this week, and the bigger one is a win: our AI-answer visibility broke out of the basement. Amplitude ran a fresh report on 7/28 (the first new run since 7/21) and Reveal's AI-answer share of voice jumped from 4.7% to 27.4%, with average rank improving 9.8 → 7.9. Reveal now sits above Qlik Sense (22.6%), Zoho and AWS — no longer last. That directly answers the top weakness we flagged the past two weeks; the comparison-content and listicle-seeding bet is showing up in the data. (Caveat: the prompt panel shrank from 253 to 124 responses this cycle, so treat the magnitude as directional — but the direction, and clearing Qlik, is real.)

The offsetting move is competitive: Luzmo shipped a July release (Luzmo IQ via Studio + a downloadable "Agent Skills" pack of 10 skills / 4 subagents for AI coding tools like Cursor and Claude Code) and is the only pure-play gaining organic traffic (~+12% WoW). Luzmo is now out-shipping us on the exact AI-agent surface we don't yet answer. Everything else held: Explo's decay stabilized (−38% last week, roughly flat this week — the migration window is open but no longer accelerating), Domo→Progress is unchanged (signed 7/22, closes by Nov 30), and no competitor ran new PR or funding. Net: bank the AI-visibility win, and treat the AI-agent product gap as the thing to close before the next launch cycle.

4Our AI-answer visibility — the breakout
Reveal BI's AI-answer share of voice jumped from 4.7% to 27.4% on Amplitude's fresh 7/28 run (report #187963) — mentioned in 34 of 124 LLM responses, average rank improved to 7.9 (from 9.8), across 445 citations and 37.9% prompt coverage. Critically, Reveal now sits above Qlik Sense (22.6%), Zoho (4.8%) and AWS (3.2%) and is no longer bottom of the tracked set: Microsoft 57.3%, Looker 55.6%, Salesforce 49.2%, Metabase 45.2%, Sisense 39.5%, then Reveal. The 8-week trend (10.7 → 6.5 → 10.2 → 6.2 → 9.1 → 4.7 → 27.4) has been volatile, and the response panel shrank from 253 to 124 this cycle — so part of the jump is methodology. But the direction is clearly up and clearing Qlik is a real threshold. Implication: the comparison-content + listicle-seeding work flagged the past two weeks is landing in LLM answers. Keep feeding it and re-confirm on the next run before declaring a durable gain.
