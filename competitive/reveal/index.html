1What changed since last week
A quiet news week with one loud data signal: the SEO leaderboard among the embedded pure-plays just flipped. Reveal's estimated organic traffic jumped to ~8,700/mo while Explo's collapsed ~38% to ~6,100/mo — so Reveal is now the #1 organic-traffic pure-play, overtaking Explo for the first time. This is exactly the decay we predicted last week as Explo's roadmap freezes into the Omni sunset; it's now visible in the numbers. (Caveat: part of Reveal's jump looks like an Ahrefs model refresh — treat the direction as solid, the magnitude as directional.)

On the competitor side, nothing net-new shipped: Domo→Progress is still pending (unchanged, closes by Nov 30), and the Qrvey 9.4 / Luzmo MCP-server releases are now standing facts, not news. Our AI-answer share of voice did not recover — it sits flat at 4.7% because Amplitude has not run a new report since 7/21; we're still bottom of the tracked set. Net: this is an execution week for us, not a reaction week. The competitive field paused; we should sprint on the Explo displacement and AI-visibility gaps already on the board.
