VP Readout — 30-second view
Goal: win marketing dashboards & reporting vs Databox · DashThis · AgencyAnalytics · Looker · Power BI
Fix

We're near-invisible in AI answers — now measured at 3.1%

First AI-answer baseline is in: Slingshot shows up in 3.1% of tracked LLM responses (1 of 17 prompts, avg rank ~9.7). Databox & Supermetrics sit at ~21.9% and the work-mgmt majors at 35–47%. As buyers ask ChatGPT "best marketing dashboard," we're not in the room.

Working

Act on the insight in the same tool — still ours alone

Every rival stops at the dashboard; Slingshot turns a metric into a task or a discussion in one place, with a genuine $0 free tier while the field meters by source, widget and token. No competitor has put this loop on a roadmap.

Watch

AgencyAnalytics finalizes all-in-one per-client pricing

New signups now hit a 2-tier Core ($20–25/client, every feature and all 85+ integrations, no gating) + Enterprise. It removes the "which tier has X" friction and hardens their agency lock-in. Databox MCP + Power BI/Looker metering still shape the AI-access and pricing story.

1What changed this week
Two things actually moved; the footprint did not. First, our AI-answer visibility is now baselined — Slingshot appears in 3.1% of tracked LLM responses (Amplitude AI Visibility, report completed Jul 28), versus Databox and Supermetrics at ~21.9% and work-management majors (Asana, monday.com, HubSpot) at 35–47% in the same prompt set. Last week this was "pending"; now it's a hard number, and it's low. Second, AgencyAnalytics finalized its per-client pricing migration to a 2-tier Core ($20–25/client, all features + all 85+ integrations, no gating) + Enterprise model, removing the feature-tier friction that used to slow their deals. Everything else — Databox's MCP server, DashThis source-based pricing, Power BI Fabric/Copilot metering, Looker Studio's free-but-thin AI — is standing context, not news. Organic and paid footprints are flat week-over-week. Our wedge is unchanged and still uncontested: the insight and the work live in the same tool, free to start.

2Where we can actually win search — and AI answers
Not "marketing dashboard" (saturated by DashThis/Databox) — instead the differentiated long tail: "marketing dashboard with task management", "turn dashboards into tasks", "act on marketing data", "free marketing dashboard tool", and pricing-contrast + "alternative" terms ("Databox alternative free", "DashThis alternative free", "AgencyAnalytics alternative"). These match our wedge and the metered-pricing backlash, the incumbents don't optimize for them, and — critically this week — they are exactly the pages LLMs cite when answering "best/free marketing dashboard tool," where we currently register 3.1%.
3The compounding cost of invisibility

Comparison + "alternative" pages plus a review drive attack all three at once — and here they double as demand-capture against AgencyAnalytics' paid dominance, which we can't match dollar-for-dollar.

Verdict — the one response that matters this week

Start competing for AI answers — we now know we're at 3.1% and it's costing us discovery. The footprint didn't move and our wedge is intact, but the new baseline is the signal to act on: buyers increasingly ask an LLM for the best or free marketing dashboard, and we're almost never named while Databox sits at ~22% and the work-mgmt majors at 35–47%. The fix is the same content that wins our differentiated SEO and neutralizes AgencyAnalytics' pricing reset — LLM-citable "alternative," "free," and "act on your data" pages, wrapped around the one thing no reporting tool has: a dashboard number that becomes a task and a discussion in the same tool, starting free. Make that loop the headline, make it citable, and measure the AI-answer trend every week.
