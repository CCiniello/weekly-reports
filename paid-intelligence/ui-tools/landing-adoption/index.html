3 Top 3 findings this run
1. Paid has zero provable fingerprint on React/Angular/Blazor product engagement
59 sample-app opens, trial starts, and whitepaper downloads fired in Salesforce this window across the three frameworks. Zero carry a cpc medium tag — every one is organic, direct, referral, or event-sourced. This isn't proof paid isn't contributing (React's trial flow is intentionally ungated per the open KPI task), but Salesforce currently can't confirm the link either way for any of the three frameworks.
2. Ultimate cpc trials look like +500% — half of that is a burst that needs a bot check
6 Ultimate cpc trials in the complete week of Jul 27–Aug 2 vs 1 the week before. But 3 of the 6 fired within a 5-minute window on Jul 30 under an untagged "(not set)" campaign. Excluding that cluster, the real move is 1→3 — still a genuine increase, and High confidence. New task filed to clear the cluster before anyone cites the headline number.
3. First hard evidence this run that framework pages feed the Ultimate funnel
A Jul 27 Ultimate trial traced to a YouTube video on the React page (campaign "ignite-ui-react"), and an Aug 2 Ultimate newsletter signup traced to Bing cpc on the Blazor compare page. Two data points, not a trend yet, but the first attributable proof this run that React/Blazor traffic reaches the Ultimate suite.

D Definitions & methodology
Fresh / Partial / Stale / Unavailable / Carried
Per the marketing constitution: Fresh = pulled live this run. Partial = pulled live but with a material caveat (e.g. confidence-flagged records). Stale = old data, still labeled with its true date. Unavailable = not pulled, not estimated. Carried = a prior real value quoted forward with its original date, never re-verified.
cpc-tagged
Salesforce Lead_Event__c record where TrackingMedium__c = "cpc" (case-insensitive), per the attribution-normalization brain. Absence of a cpc tag is a measurement statement, not proof paid had zero effect.
Combined npm
Sum of daily downloads (api.npmjs.org) across the named packages for a given Mon–Sun calendar week. React combined = igniteui-react + -core + -grids + -charts. Angular combined = igniteui-angular + -core + -charts. This matches the figure Poojitha published in Slingshot's Performance discussion on Aug 5 (verified: our Jul 27–Aug 2 total of 9,688 matches that post exactly).
Attribution confidence
High / Medium / Low per attribution-normalization.md. The Jul 30 Ultimate trial cluster is held at Medium/Low pending the bot/duplicate audit and is excluded from the headline trend number until cleared.
Segments
React, Angular, Blazor, Ultimate, Unclassified — classified by ProductFamilyName__c / TrackingCampaign__c contains-logic. Reveal and other non-Ignite-UI product families sharing the same Google Ads/Salesforce accounts are excluded, never blended in.
