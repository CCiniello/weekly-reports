1 Domain health read
Site health is 45/100, down 3 points since the June crawl, and the direction is wrong. Of 30,233 internal URLs crawled, 16,712 (55%) now carry at least one error — up +1,861 in one crawl. The whole thing is driven by a single failure mode: broken pages. 4XX responses jumped +1,839 to 4,358 and hard 404s jumped +1,241 to 1,799, while 5XX server errors sit at 2,000. Ahrefs' rolled-up "4xx/5xx" bucket now touches 8,253 URLs — more than a quarter of the crawled site. This is a corporate-wide crawl (every subdomain: www, jp, ko, pt-br, account, blogs, community forums), so the raw counts are large by nature — but the trend and the concentration in broken pages are the story, not the volume.

Verdict — fix this first

Stop the broken-page bleed. The one thing that matters this week is the +1,839 jump in 4XX pages (+1,241 net-new 404s), which pulled site health from 48 to 45 and pushed the rolled-up 4XX/5XX bucket to 8,253 URLs — 27% of the crawled site. A single crawl gaining ~1,800 broken pages, including a live /privacy-policy 404, points at one upstream cause — a bulk unpublish or a broken URL template — not 1,800 unrelated breakages. Find that root cause, restore or 301 the URLs, and the health score, the internal-links-to-broken-pages count, and the sitemap 4XX errors all recover together. Everything else on this list is secondary until the 404 curve turns back down.
