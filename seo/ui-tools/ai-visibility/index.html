Material UI & Ant Design — the open-source default36.6% / 31.7%

The problem is scale: those 7 sit inside 335 total citations in the report, the vast majority going to third-party "best of" listicles (adminlte.io, builder.io, hashbyt.com, tailgrids, dev.to, Reddit) that mostly omit Ignite UI. By engine, we do better on Google AI Overview (9.5%) than ChatGPT (5%) — and Gemini and Claude aren't in this report at all.

Verdict — the one move this week

Get Ignite UI into the high-volume third-party "best of" listicles that LLMs cite most — starting with the enterprise/data-grid lists that already rank our competitors. Our own blogs are the only thing surfacing us, and self-citation caps out fast; the answer real estate on the head prompts (enterprise UI libraries, react datagrid rankings, excel-like grids, complex dashboards — 480–2,400 searches each) is decided by neutral listicles where we're absent but DevExpress/Kendo/Syncfusion are present. Prioritize outreach/placement on builder.io, hashbyt.com, adminlte.io, faciletechnolab and tailgrids, and in parallel expand our proven owned comparison pages to target the five highest-volume gap prompts.
