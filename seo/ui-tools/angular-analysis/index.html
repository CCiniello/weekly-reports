1Executive summary
Angular organic held steady and ticked up: modeled organic traffic rose to 1,610/mo (+4.5% WoW) and the top-3 footprint grew to 134 keywords (+4). Rankings are the source of truth this week; GA4 behavior is directional at best until tracking is fixed.

Verdict — where to invest next

Push the "data binding in angular" cluster and fix analytics — in that order. The two-way-binding guide is the highest-leverage asset on the property: it just cracked page one at #8 for a 1,200-volume, KD-1 term and already leads all Angular pages at 162 visits. Tighten its title/H1 around "data binding," add internal links from the grid and forms docs, and it should reach the top 3 for a likely +100 visits/mo. Second, close the GA4 blind spot — the component/docs pages that Ahrefs credits with nearly all our traffic register almost nothing in GA4, so we can't measure engagement or conversions where the audience actually is; get the docs app onto this stream. Third, defend charts: refresh chart-overview to stop "angular chart" (5→8) sliding further. Rankings are healthy and growing; the growth ceiling this quarter is measurement, not search.
