For Dean — CEO briefing

Slingshot SEO & AI Visibility — where we are

The 60-second version. We're rebuilding Slingshot's search presence on the site we already own rather than starting over — the domain has real authority (1,263 referring domains) that would take roughly two years to rebuild from scratch. The catch: that authority is pointed at the wrong pages. We get about 179,000 Google impressions a week, but we sit around position 33 and really only get clicked for our own brand name. None of the six pages we want to win on rank yet. For the second week of a reset — a build week, laying foundations rather than chasing quick wins — that's exactly the expected starting line.

What we're doing about it

We start with comparison pages — "Slingshot vs Asana," "AgencyAnalytics alternative" — because that's where a buyer is closest to choosing, and where we can win fastest (we already rank on page 2 for one of them). From there we build the three pages a marketing buyer cares about most — analytics, campaign management, project management — and then define the category itself so Slingshot gets named when someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity for the best tool. This week's five priority moves finish the comparison migration and stand up two brand-new hubs — marketing-analytics and campaign-management — the two topics where our AI visibility is currently 0%.

Where the numbers stand

Target-page rankings: 0 of 6 (expected this early). From organic search this week we drove 0 demo requests and 1 self-serve first login for Slingshot — tiny, but now tracked live from Salesforce and separated the way our product-led model actually works. Referring domains are up 2. AI share of voice reads 3.1% (Amplitude) vs Asana ~47% and monday ~41% — but that is a stale monthly figure (last crawled Jul 28), flat rather than freshly earned. Two honest flags: our tracked keyword footprint contracted (461→409 keywords, 127→106 in the top three) as authority stays split across duplicate pages — the consolidation fixes this. Domain Rating holds at 55. The core tension: impressions rose 18% while clicks stayed flat — we show up more but rank too deep, on the wrong queries. Moving our money pages up is the whole game.

What I need from you

Nothing this week. One heads-up: when we restructure the URLs in a few weeks, rankings typically dip for 4–8 weeks before recovering higher — that's normal for this kind of rebuild, and I'll flag it in advance so a temporary dip isn't misread as the program failing.

Strategy quick-reference. The playbook behind the report in one place — the pillars we’re building, the two-track rule that governs the week, the guardrails we never break, and a plain-English glossary for the numbers. Anything tagged Ours is a Slingshot-specific definition, not a generic industry term.

The six pillars

1 · Comparisons & Alternatives start here
Highest intent, lowest difficulty. We already rank #14 for "agencyanalytics alternative"; 14 /slingshot-vs-*/ pages exist. Consolidate under /compare/ — the fastest qualified traffic and the pages AI cites for "X vs Y".
2 · Marketing Analytics & Reporting
The head-term demand a revenue-accountable marketer searches for — "marketing analytics platform / reporting / dashboards". New hub /marketing-analytics/ absorbs 3 dupes.
3 · Campaign Management
"campaign management software" (1,000/mo, KD 8) + "marketing calendar software". New hub /campaign-management/ absorbs 3 scattered pages.
4 · Marketing Project Management
/marketing-project-management/ already exists — optimize and consolidate, don’t rebuild.
5 · Discipline Workflows
SEO / content / social workflows — our on-ICP toehold (we rank #7–10 for SEO workflow). Consolidate the two SEO pages into one.
6 · Combined Category + AI create demand
Define and own "where data and work live together" and seed the thought-leadership that earns AI citations.

The two-track rule

Lane 1 · Content
Ship and optimize the money pages in pillar order — comparisons first, then the analytics/campaign/PM hubs, then the category pillar. This is where rankings and qualified traffic come from.
Lane 2 · Technical / Cleanup
Crawl hygiene runs in parallel — 301 maps, trailing-slash convention, locale/hreflang, schema, sitemaps, prune + llms.txt. Clean plumbing so the content work actually ranks. Both lanes advance every week (see the Roadmap tab).

Guardrails

3–5 moves a week
The agent picks the 3–5 highest-leverage actions each week and works them to done — no sprawling backlog.
URL law
Lowercase, hyphenated, one canonical form; money pages max two levels deep.
301, never delete
Every removed URL gets a 301 to its replacement so link authority carries over — never a hard delete.
ICP focus
Effort goes to the marketing money terms. Huge off-ICP demand ("task management", "data analysis") is watched, not funded.
Connector write limit flag
Slingshot Website custom fields (Search Intent, Funnel, Webpage Type) and the time-estimate are not yet writable via the MCP connector, so those values are surfaced in the report/brief (Tasks tab) until the connector supports writes.

Glossary — the numbers

AI Share of Voice (AI SoV) monthly crawl
How often AI answer engines cite Slingshot across a fixed set of category prompts, as a % of answers. 3.1% this run — a monthly crawl (last run Jul 28), so it reads as unchanged, not freshly measured.
AI avg rank
Average position we hold within an AI answer when cited (1 = first-named). Ours is 9.7 — near the bottom.
AI coverage by topic Ours
% of answers that include Slingshot, by topic. Comparisons is our best (10%); Campaign management and Marketing Analytics & Reporting are 0% — the gaps the new hubs target.
Impressions / clicks (GSC)
Impressions = times a Slingshot result showed in Google; clicks = times it was clicked. Impressions +18% with flat clicks means we show more but rank too deep to earn the click.
Average position
Mean ranking of our results in Google (1 = top). 33 ≈ page 3–4 — visible in impression counts, invisible to most searchers.
Top-3 & organic keywords
Front-page terms fell 127→106; total ranking keywords fell 461→409 — footprint contraction, usually from authority splitting across duplicate pages.
Domain Rating (DR) & referring domains
DR = Ahrefs 0–100 backlink-strength score; ours is 55, steady. Referring domains = distinct sites linking to us, up to 1,263 (+2).
Content hub & cannibalization Ours
A hub is one authoritative page that absorbs thin overlapping pages so signals concentrate on one URL. Cannibalization is when multiple pages compete for the same query and none rank — collapsing the 6 dupes into two hubs fixes it.
Organic demo & first-login Ours
Organic demo = a demo request attributed to organic search (0 this week, expected in a groundwork phase). First-login = a new user’s first sign-in, an early activation signal (1 this week).
