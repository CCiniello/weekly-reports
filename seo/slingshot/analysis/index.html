VP readout — for Dean

Week two of the reset is a build week, not a numbers week — and the numbers say exactly that.

Search demand for us actually grew: Google showed our pages 179k times last week, up 18%, even as clicks held flat at 212 and organic sessions kept drifting down (151/wk). That gap is the reset in one line — Google is indexing and surfacing more of our footprint, but we're surfacing deep (avg position 33) on pages that aren't yet consolidated, so the impressions don't convert to clicks. The fix is already in motion.

This week we did the thing that moves that number: we put five P1 jobs on the board and, for the first time, pushed and assigned them in Slingshot (the connector that was down last week is back). Three finish the comparison migration that stalled; two stand up the /marketing-analytics/ and /campaign-management/ hubs and collapse six cannibalizing pages into them. Those two hubs are the exact topics where AI answers score us 0% today — you can't be cited on "campaign management software" when three of your own pages are splitting the vote.

Honest flags: our Ahrefs keyword footprint contracted week-over-week (461→409 keywords, 127→106 in the top three) and AI share-of-voice is still 3.1% — but the AI figure is a monthly crawl that hasn't re-run since Jul 28, and the keyword dip is expected volatility on a low baseline before the consolidations ship. Referring domains ticked up to 1,263 and DR holds at 55. Still zero organic demos; one organic first-login. We are pre-revenue on organic and building the plumbing that earns it.

Analysis

Cannibalization is the throttle. Three internal pages compete for marketing-analytics intent and three more for campaign intent — none ranks, and the split authority is why we're invisible on both topics in Google and in AI answers. Consolidating each cluster into one canonical hub (this week's moves 4–5) is the single highest-leverage action available; it fixes search and AEO at once.

The impressions/clicks gap. +18% impressions with flat clicks and a deepening average position means Google is testing us on more queries but ranking us on page 3–4. That's normal mid-consolidation — thin, duplicative pages get shown but not clicked. Expect this to invert once the 301s land the equity on single strong URLs.

Content cleanup. GA4 confirms the off-ICP magnets bounce: /blog/top-task-management-tools/ engaged just 17% of sessions (18s avg), while the on-ICP winner /blog/types-of-data-analysis/ engaged 75% (524s). Re-angle or 301 the bounce magnets; internal-link the winners up to the money hubs. Queued for next week to hold WIP.
