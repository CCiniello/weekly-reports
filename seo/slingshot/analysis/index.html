For Dean — CEO briefing

Slingshot SEO & AI Visibility — where we are

The 60-second version. We're rebuilding Slingshot's search presence on the site we already own rather than starting over — the domain has real authority (1,225 referring domains) that would take roughly two years to rebuild from scratch. The catch: that authority is pointed at the wrong pages. We get about 150,000 Google impressions a week, but we sit around position 33 and really only get clicked for our own brand name. None of the six pages we want to win on rank yet. For week one of a reset, that's exactly the expected starting line.

What we're doing about it

We start with comparison pages — "Slingshot vs Asana," "AgencyAnalytics alternative" — because that's where a buyer is closest to choosing, and where we can win fastest (we already rank on page 2 for one of them, and 14 of these pages already exist to consolidate). From there we build the three pages a marketing buyer cares about most — analytics, campaign management, project management — and then define the category itself so Slingshot gets named when someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity for the best tool.

Where the numbers stand

Target-page rankings: 0 of 6 (expected this early). From organic search this week we drove 0 demo requests and 1 self-serve first login for Slingshot — tiny, but now tracked live from Salesforce and separated the way our product-led model actually works. Referring domains are up 56. AI share-of-voice: baseline pending. The honest tension is lots of impressions but almost no clicks — we rank too deep, on the wrong queries. Moving our money pages up is the whole game.

What I need from you

Nothing this week. One heads-up: when we restructure the URLs in a few weeks, rankings typically dip for 4–8 weeks before recovering higher — that's normal for this kind of rebuild, and I'll flag it in advance so a temporary dip isn't misread as the program failing.
