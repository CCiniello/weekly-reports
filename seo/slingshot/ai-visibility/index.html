Metrics are flat on purpose — no new crawl ran this window — so we stayed at 3.1% visibility, one mention across 17 buyer questions, while Asana, Monday, and ClickUp own the answers. The value this week was in the plan getting sharper, not the score.

When someone asks ChatGPT "Slingshot vs Asana," we win — ranked first and described exactly as we position ourselves: work and analytics in one. And the one blog we wrote in an answer-first format is still the only Slingshot page any AI engine cites. The playbook is proven; this week we double down on it — turning that blog into a category hub and cloning its format into vs-Asana and vs-Monday pages.

Two things, both now owned as tasks. AI tools confuse our name with the Polaris Slingshot vehicle and a movie, so branded questions misfire — a schema fix that's in flight. And on the big non-branded questions ("best marketing analytics tool"), AI builds its answer from third-party "best of" lists that don't include us. This week we pulled the exact list of those pages from the data, so the PR push is now named and targeted. We're also completely absent from Google's AI Overviews (0 of 15) — that's now its own task.

What happens next

Eleven tasks are live and owned — six carried over from last week (in flight) plus five new, data-targeted this week. Priorities: ship the vs-Asana / vs-Monday pages and the MEP hub, land Slingshot on the named listicles, fix the entity confusion, and retune the tracked questions toward the money pillars. Targets once the next crawl runs: visibility above 5%, comparison-topic visibility to 15%, our first Google-AI-Overview mention, and movement up the share-of-voice ranking.
