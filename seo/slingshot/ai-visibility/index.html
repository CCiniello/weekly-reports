Executive Summary

Our single mention came from "Slingshot vs Asana" on ChatGPT, where Slingshot ranked #1 and was correctly framed as a unified work-plus-analytics workspace. Separately, our blog /blog/what-is-a-marketing-execution-platform was the only Slingshot-owned page cited in the entire report — pulled into Google's AI Overview for "what is a marketing execution platform," though the answer didn't name us. That page is the template: it leads with an answer-first executive summary and an FAQ, which is exactly what answer engines extract.

Two problems cap us. First, an entity-resolution failure: "Is Slingshot any good?" on Google returns the Polaris Slingshot vehicle, the 2024 movie, and a NZ internet provider — not us. Second, zero presence in non-branded money queries: every "best marketing analytics / campaign / PM software" answer is assembled from third-party listicles (Whatagraph, Databox, DashThis, AgencyAnalytics, thecmo.com, G2, Wrike/Asana/monday resource hubs) that don't mention Slingshot. Closing that off-site gap is the biggest lever we have.

Executive summary

AI Search Visibility — Slingshot

The one-liner: This is our first read on whether Slingshot shows up when marketers ask AI tools for software recommendations. Today we barely do — 3.1% visibility, one mention across 17 buyer questions — while Asana, Monday, and ClickUp own the answers. That's the expected starting line for a category we're building, and we now have a clear plan to climb it.

What's working

When someone asks ChatGPT "Slingshot vs Asana," we win — ranked first and described exactly as we position ourselves: work and analytics in one place. And the one blog we wrote in an answer-first format got pulled into Google's AI Overview. The playbook is proven; we just haven't run it at scale yet.

What's holding us back

Two things. AI tools confuse our name with the Polaris Slingshot vehicle and a 2024 movie, so branded questions misfire — a fixable technical problem. And on the big non-branded questions ("best marketing analytics tool"), AI builds its answer from third-party "best of" lists that don't include Slingshot yet. Getting onto those lists is the single biggest lever.

What happens next

Six tasks are queued and owned: retune the tracked questions, fix the entity confusion with schema, populate our (currently empty) llms.txt, upgrade our comparison and solution pages to the format AI actually quotes, and start a PR push to land Slingshot in the lists LLMs cite. Targets for next week: visibility above 5%, comparison-topic visibility to 15%, and our first movement up the share-of-voice ranking.
