Slingshot technical SEO & cleanup — week of Jul 20–26

Health is steady at 46/100, our baseline, with no regressions. Week-over-week the crawl dropped 44 error URLs and 287 notice URLs while the score held — the expected shape of a pre-migration hygiene phase, where we clear surface debris before the equity-moving fixes land.

What we're fixing this week, and why. Five things, all foundation-critical. We're picking a single URL/slash convention and 301-redirecting the trailing-slash duplicates that currently split ranking signals across ~1,600 redirect hops; repointing 568 broken redirects and 180 mis-set canonicals so link equity stops leaking; consolidating our two competing SEO pages into one and collapsing campaign-management from three URLs into one canonical hub; and fixing the revealbi.io references bleeding onto our own demo and contact forms. In short: stop the site competing with itself and stop equity draining out of broken plumbing — before we touch the taxonomy.

Migration status. We're in Phase 0→1 (foundation and consolidation). The full new-taxonomy redirect map gets built and staged in ~2 weeks, then launched in one controlled push around week 6, with daily 404/coverage monitoring for two weeks after.

Please read this before the numbers wobble: an IA migration of this kind normally produces a 4–8 week dip in rankings and organic traffic as Google reprocesses the redirects, followed by recovery above baseline. That dip is planned and healthy — not a sign the program is failing. We'll report ICP-qualified traffic and demos separately from raw sessions so the real signal isn't lost in the noise.

One risk to close now: Google Search Console returned no coverage/404 data through our tooling this week. Daily GSC monitoring is the safety net for the redirect launch, so we need the GSC connection confirmed and flowing before week 6 — otherwise we'd be migrating blind.
