Executive summary

Reveal's AI answer visibility improved on quality but narrowed on breadth this week. Our visibility score more than doubled to 27.4% and average rank improved to 7.9 from 9.3, and both tracked models moved up — Google AI Overview to 37.9% (from 15.7%) and ChatGPT to 18.2% (from 7.9%). But the sample was one-third the size of last week (124 responses vs 367), our absolute mentions slipped from 43 to 34, and we now appear in 37.9% of prompts, down from 48.5%. Translation: we're ranking better where we show up, but we're showing up in fewer places. The engine of our strength is unchanged — comparison and alternatives content (77% visibility) — and the drag is unchanged too: we're absent from the "best/top embedded analytics" listicles and Reddit threads that models cite for discovery queries, and the tracker still isn't measuring our real competitive set. The plan is to press the comparison advantage into the peer set (Explo/Qrvey/Domo pages), earn citations in the third-party lists models actually quote, and fix the Amplitude configuration so we're measuring the race we're trying to win.
