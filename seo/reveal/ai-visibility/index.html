Executive summary

On the now-stable measurement config, Reveal's AI answer visibility held and edged up: overall visibility 32.3% (from 31.3%), avg rank 7.6 (from 7.5), prompt coverage 44.4% (from 43.2%), and 624 citations (from 560), across 155 responses. The engine is unchanged — comparison and alternatives content (76% visibility, rank 3.3) — and so is the drag: we score 0% on "Embedded analytics platforms" despite 95/100 relevance, and 10% on "SaaS & ISV," because third-party listicles drive those answers and mostly omit us. Two model families flipped this week (Google AI Overview down to 39.2% from 45.7%; ChatGPT up to 25.9% from 18.8%). The realignment that fixed most of the competitor set is Done and validated — Luzmo, Qrvey, Bold BI, GoodData, Embeddable and Sigma are now measured, and Reveal sits 6th of 14 — but it left two gaps: Explo and Domo aren't tracked, and only 2 of 5 charter models are. The plan: finish the tracker config, press the comparison advantage into Explo/Qrvey/Domo pages, and earn third-party citations in the "best embedded analytics" roundups models actually quote.
