Plain-English SEO glossary

Keyword
A phrase people type into search. We group related keywords into "clusters."
Head vs long-tail
Head = short, high-volume, competitive ("embedded bi"). Long-tail = longer, lower-volume, easier ("best white label analytics platforms").
Search volume (Vol)
Estimated monthly searches for a keyword (Ahrefs) — the size of the demand.
Ranking page (GSC)
The specific URL Google is currently ranking for that keyword.
Impressions
How many times a result was shown in search.
Clicks & CTR
Clicks = visits from search. CTR = clicks ÷ impressions. High impressions + low CTR = shown but not chosen.
Position / page 1 vs page 2
Average rank. #1–10 = page 1 (gets nearly all clicks); #11–20 = page 2 (almost none).
Backlink
A link from another site to ours — a vote of authority.
Domain Rating (DR)
0–100 score of overall backlink strength.
Canonical
The tag naming the "official" version of a page so duplicates don't compete.
Technical health
Audit score for how clean the site is to crawl (errors, redirects, broken links).
AI visibility (AEO)
Answer Engine Optimization — being cited in AI answers (ChatGPT, Google AI Overview).
Share of voice
Our visibility vs competitors across the same prompts or queries.
Cluster / PAGE GAP
Cluster = a group of related terms mapped to one page. PAGE GAP = terms with demand but no owned page yet.
The one rule: one metric, one tool. If two tools disagree, they're measuring different things — we don't reconcile them, and we never fabricate a number.
